# Flame AI-Sales — Sales Analytics Concept

**Category:** Business analytics and machine learning  
**Status:** Public repository / project implementation  
**Type:** Flame Connect product exploration

## Overview
A Streamlit-based sales intelligence application designed to turn uploaded sales datasets into more useful business insights.

## Selected capabilities
- CSV data ingestion and field mapping.
- Revenue forecasting experiments using machine-learning approaches.
- Lead scoring, filtered views, and executive-style summaries.
- Data exploration without requiring users to write Python.

## Technology
Python, Streamlit, data processing, and machine-learning libraries.
