# Flame Connect — AI & Growth Tools Platform

**Category:** AI readiness, sales intelligence, and business process tools  
**Status:** Alpha / in development; privately maintained  
**Type:** Flame Connect product

## Overview
An Africa-first collection of AI-readiness, business analytics, sales-practice, and growth-support tools under the Flame Connect product direction.

## Selected capabilities
- AI readiness and digital-growth assessment concepts.
- Guided playbooks and learning tools.
- Structured data intake and analytics workspaces.
- Governance-oriented design: explainable scoring, human review, and separation of organizations' data.

## Technology
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase-oriented architecture, and governed AI integration patterns.

## Access
The development repository is private. Demonstrations and screenshots may be shared separately when appropriate.

> Development status: Source/build validation does not mean the platform is fully production-ready or commercially available.
