## Who we are

**Flame Connect** is a Uganda-based digital technology initiative focused on helping people and organizations use software, data, AI, and thoughtful digital experiences to solve practical business problems.

This portfolio brings together **Flame Connect products and selected work by its founder in other professional roles**. Not every project shown was a Flame Connect commercial engagement. Project status and access are made clear below.

## What we build

- **Websites & web applications:** Responsive business sites, user experiences, and custom application interfaces.
- **AI & data solutions:** Readiness tools, data exploration, predictive-analytics experiments, and human-reviewed intelligence workflows.
- **Business process automation:** Digital workflows that reduce repetitive work and support more consistent operations.
- **Software prototypes & MVPs:** Early-stage solutions to test an idea, demonstrate workflows, and prepare for further engineering.
- **Digital consulting:** Practical guidance on technology adoption, user experience, and digital transformation.

Private repositories, confidential customer information, internal screenshots, credentials, and proprietary specifications are excluded. Named client branding, operational demonstrations, and detailed screenshots should be added only with the relevant owner's consent.

A project labelled **prototype**, **alpha**, or **MVP** should not be mistaken for a production-ready paid service. Public code availability also does not, by itself, establish an open-source licence.
