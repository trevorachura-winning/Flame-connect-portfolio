# Corporate Learning Management System (LMS)

**Category:** Learning technology / internal enterprise software  
**Status:** Private MVP / professional engineering engagement  
**Type:** Work by the portfolio owner in a professional role; not presented as a Flame Connect client contract

## Overview
A corporate LMS project focused on staff training delivery, learning access, administration, and progress tracking.

## Selected capabilities
- Role-based learning and administrative experiences.
- Course and lesson organization.
- Learner enrollment and progress-oriented workflows.
- Authentication and organization-scoped data controls.

## Technology
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase Authentication, PostgreSQL, and row-level security.
