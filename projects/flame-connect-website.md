# Flame Connect — Corporate Website

**Category:** Corporate website and digital experience  
**Status:** Public source repository; deployment status should be checked independently  
**Type:** Flame Connect product

## Overview
A responsive, mobile-first web experience introducing Flame Connect's services, products, resources, and responsible-technology approach, designed for audiences in Uganda and beyond.

## Selected capabilities
- Service and product information pages with explicit product-status labels.
- Resource and insights content.
- Contact enquiry flow and accessibility-conscious interactions.
- Content-integrity checks and responsive design.

## Technology
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, modern frontend styling, and a deployment workflow suitable for Vercel.
