# Recruitment & Applicant Tracking Portal

**Category:** Human resources technology / web application  
**Status:** Private MVP / professional engineering engagement  
**Type:** Work by the portfolio owner in a professional role; not presented as a Flame Connect client contract

## Overview
A recruitment workflow platform intended to improve how vacancies, candidate applications, and recruiter activities are coordinated.

## Selected capabilities
- Candidate account and application workflows.
- Job listings and application handling.
- Recruiter-oriented management interfaces.
- Authorization-conscious design around candidate and staff access.

## Technology
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, and Vercel-oriented deployment workflows.
